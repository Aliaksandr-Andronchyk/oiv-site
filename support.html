Frequently asked

The orb doesn’t hear me

Make sure you granted microphone and speech-recognition permission. Open iOS Settings → OIV and enable both Microphone and Speech Recognition, then relaunch the app.

Does it work without internet?

Speech recognition runs on your device. Tap the orb, speak, and OIV reads a reply back.

The conversation stopped on the Lock Screen

OIV keeps the dialog going via a Live Activity. If it stopped, make sure Live Activities are enabled in Settings → Face ID & Passcode / Lock Screen and that Low Power Mode isn’t suspending background audio.

How do I clear my history?

Conversation history is stored only on your device. Clear it from within the app, or delete the app to remove everything.
