Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 6, 2026

OIV (“the app”) is a voice-first companion. We designed it to collect as little as possible. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

Speech & microphone

OIV requests microphone and speech-recognition permission only to power the live voice dialog. Speech recognition runs on your device using Apple’s on-device speech framework. The app does not require an account, and does not upload, sell, or share recordings of your voice for advertising.

What we don’t collect

  • No account or sign-up is required to use the core voice dialog.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your conversation history is stored locally on your device so you can review past dialogs. You can clear it at any time from within the app or by deleting the app.

Subscriptions

If you purchase a subscription, the transaction is handled by Apple’s App Store and (where applicable) a subscription-management provider. We receive only the entitlement status needed to unlock features — not your payment details.

Children

OIV is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new “last updated” date.
